Artist Statement

Preserving Fleeting Atmospheric Moments Across the Bengal Delta

Khonchitro (ক্ষণচিত্র) translates to the fleeting sketch or ephemeral moment. Rooted in the deltaic landscapes of Bangladesh, our photographic practice examines the interplay between water, memory, and time.

In a region where geography is continuously reshaped by monsoons and river currents, photographs do not merely freeze time—they document resilience, quiet grace, and the sacred relationships between communities and their waterways.
